## What is EDIS authentication?

- EDIS(Electronic Delivery Instruction Slip) is a facility which allows you to sell shares when you have not submitted your Power of Attorney(POA).
- You can only sell the stocks you are holding in your linked Demat account. You may submit your POA to ensure a seamless trading experience.
- EDIS flow is applicable only for Delivery Sell orders which orders should have holdings available in holding section.

## What is GSM scrips?

- GSM (Graded Surveillance Measure) refers to a specific type of surveillance measure used by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) to monitor and regulate trading activities in certain securities.
- It's designed to alert investors and market participants about potential risks associated with specific stocks, particularly those showing unusual price movements or trading patterns that don't reflect the company's underlying fundamentals.

## What is Banned scrips?

- A stock ban in Future and Option (F&O) occurs when the open interest (OI) in a stock's derivative contracts exceeds 95% of the market-wide position limit, also known as Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL). This limit is set by stock exchanges to prevent excessive speculation and maintain market stability.
